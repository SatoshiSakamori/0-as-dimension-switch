\begin{abstract}
In the conventional real number line, zero is positioned at the center between positive and negative values. 
This paper proposes a reinterpretation of zero as a \emph{dimension switch} --- a non-numeric boundary between two distinct spaces. 
The key feature is the \textbf{Reflection Rule} governing operations that cross the zero boundary, ensuring magnitude conservation while switching the dimensional state.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
In standard arithmetic, zero acts as the additive identity and lies at the center of symmetry in the real number line. 
This conceptual framework explores treating zero not as a number but as a transition point between two dimensions, allowing for a fresh approach to quantities that naturally conserve magnitude while possessing opposite directions.

\section{Motivation}
The primary motivation for this reinterpretation is to develop a structure that handles opposition ($\text{e.g., } a + (-a)$) as a shift in state rather than a mere cancellation to an additive identity.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Directional Conservation:} Modeling physical phenomena (e.g., mass, energy) where magnitude cannot be negative, but direction/state can be reversed (anti-matter analogy).
    \item \textbf{Algebraic Clarity:} Replacing the traditional symmetry of the real line with a \textbf{V-shaped reflection model}, where all numbers are positive in their respective space.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Definition of Spaces}
We define:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathbb{R}^+$: the \emph{positive space}, containing elements $x = (m, +1)$ where $m \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$.
    \item $\mathbb{R}^-$: the \emph{mirror space}, containing elements $x^{-} = (m, -1)$ where $m \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$.
    \item $0$: the non-numeric \emph{switch point} or \emph{boundary}, where the magnitude $m=0$.
\end{itemize}
Elements of $\mathbb{R}^-$ are symbolic, corresponding to the magnitude in the mirror dimension.

The overall structure can be conceptually viewed as:
\[
    \mathbb{S} = \left(\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \times \{+1, -1\}\right) / \sim
\]
where the equivalence relation $\sim$ identifies $(0, +1)$ and $(0, -1)$ with the single boundary point $0$.

\section{Transformation Across Zero}
We introduce a \emph{space transformation} (Dimension Switch operator):
\[
    T: \mathbb{R}^+ \to \mathbb{R}^-, \quad T((m, +1)) = (m, -1) = m^{-}
\]
and its inverse
\[
    T^{-1}: \mathbb{R}^- \to \mathbb{R}^+, \quad T^{-1}((m, -1)) = (m, +1) = m
\]
These transformations formalize the dimensional change upon crossing the boundary.

\section{Arithmetic Axioms (The Reflection Rule)}

We define the primary operation $\ominus$ (generalized subtraction) for elements $x = (m_x, s_x)$ and $y = (m_y, s_y)$, focusing on the case where $x \in \mathbb{R}^+$ and $y$ is the inverse operation (subtraction of magnitude $m_y$ towards the origin).

\subsection{Subtraction Crossing the Boundary}
If we subtract a magnitude $m_y$ from a positive element $m_x$:
$$
m_x \ominus m_y = 
\begin{cases} 
(m_x - m_y, +1) & \text{if } m_x \ge m_y \quad \text{(Stays in } \mathbb{R}^+) \\
(m_y - m_x, -1) & \text{if } m_x < m_y \quad \text{(Reflects to } \mathbb{R}^-)
\end{cases}
$$
This axiom models the "bounce" or reflection off the $0$ boundary, ensuring that the net magnitude is conserved, but the dimensional state flips.

\subsection{Continuity and Limits}
In this framework, the \textbf{magnitude} of a quantity is continuous across $0$:
$$ \lim_{t \to 0} |f(t)| = 0 $$
The discontinuity is confined only to the discrete state variable $s \in \{+1, -1\}$, not the physical quantity $m$. This addresses the ambiguity of continuity present in previous interpretations.

\section{Potential Applications}
Potential mathematical and physical applications include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Modeling systems where the absolute value (energy/mass) is conserved during a directional reversal (e.g., anti-matter creation).
    \item Providing an alternative basis for vector spaces where the directional axis is explicitly separated from the magnitude.
    \item Exploring extensions of algebra where the additive identity is replaced by a "state annihilation" or "boundary condition."
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Work and Open Questions}
Future developments may include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Rigorous algebraic formalization of multiplication and addition involving elements from both $\mathbb{R}^+$ and $\mathbb{R}^-$.
    \item Geometric representation using animation tools such as Manim.
    \item Analyzing the compatibility of this structure with existing algebraic fields.
\end{itemize}
